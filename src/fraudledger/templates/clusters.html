What this list is highlighting

A group is providers connected by a shared owner, or by the same street address when at least three providers use it. The group number is an id, not a rank.

Read the count of providers that have a billing indicator. Everyone else is in the group only because of that link. A group of several hundred is usually a corporate family, not one lead.

Open the group and use each provider’s own score. On a provider, 8 or more is Critical, 5 is High, and 3 is Elevated. A group sort key in the hundreds is not that score. The owner id on a group page is the PECOS associate id (ASSOCIATE ID – OWNER), not an NPI. How a group differs from a provider score.
